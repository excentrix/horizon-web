## The model: hub-and-spoke, founder-led

**The founder's personal brand is the engine. Company/product Pages are credibility surfaces, not
reach engines.** (Company Pages get near-zero organic reach; a personal profile gets 10–50× more.)

- **Hub = Sid (personal).** The narrative, the reach, the trust. This is where the work goes.
- **Spokes = Excentrix / VELO Pages.** "Is this a real company?" check for serious buyers (HR, colleges).
  Light touch: repost Sid's wins + a monthly product note. Never sweat their numbers.

**Branding:** rename the "Horizon" LinkedIn Page → **VELO (by Excentrix)** so it matches what people
actually sign up for. Lead with VELO; Horizon rolls in later (see VOICE.md narrative frame).

## One story, five native dialects (NEVER blanket-post)

Same *idea*, translated into each platform's "social contract" — what its audience expects:

| Platform | Audience expects | Format | Tone | CTA posture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Professional insight, career-relevant opinion | Text post / carousel | Credible, opinionated, *not* salesy | Soft, at the end |
| **X** | In-the-arena builder culture, hot takes | Threads, screenshots, real-time | Raw, fast, technical, banter OK | Light, threaded |
| **Instagram** | Entertainment, visual, short | 15–40s Reel | Energetic, hook-first, show-don't-tell | "Link in bio" |
| **Reddit** | Peer value, zero promotion | Honest text, AMA, discussion | Humble, transparent, no logo | Earned; often in a comment |
| **Blog** | Search-intent depth, the definitive answer | Long-form, structured | Authoritative, evergreen | Natural in-content |

**One real interrogation transcript → five cuts:** LinkedIn carousel (insight) · X thread (the technical
gotcha) · Reel (the 20s reaction) · Reddit write-up ("I made an AI grill me — here's what broke") ·
blog post (the canonical essay). The `/velo-content` skill does this translation.

## Narrative > marketing (the ratio)
- **~80% narrative / value / POV · ~20% soft CTA** on LinkedIn / X / IG.
- **~95 / 5 on Reddit** (insight first; link earned).
- The story *is* the marketing. People pull themselves toward the product; you never push.

## The anti-burnout workflow
1. **Batch weekly, not daily.** One 2–3 hr session: record 1–2 real interrogations (the seed asset) +
   write the week's blog post. The recording is gold — everything spins out of it.
2. **One asset → many cuts** via `/velo-content`.
3. **Schedule, don't live-post** — Buffer/Publer free tier queues a week at once.
4. **Weekly review** — paste PostHog `?ref=` numbers; double down on what *converted*, not what got likes.
