# VELO — Voice & Messaging Guide

> Feed this to any Claude session before generating content so it sounds like **Sid**, not like AI.
> Pairs with [CONTENT.md](./CONTENT.md) (the calendar) and [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md) (the thesis).

## Who's talking
Sid — a hands-on AI engineer/architect in **Bangalore** who built Horizon and is now leading with VELO.
Builder-to-builder. Credible because he ships, not because he hypes.

## Tone
- **Honest to a fault** — names what's nice-to-have vs. critical, admits limitations, shows failures.
- **Opinionated on the idea, never on people.** Attack AI-padding the practice; celebrate builders.
- **Plain and direct.** Short sentences. Real engineering specifics (file names, trade-offs) over buzzwords.
- **Conviction, not arrogance.** "Here's what I believe and why," not "we're revolutionizing."
- **Calm authority.** No hype words, no emojis-as-personality, no "🚀 thrilled to announce."

## Do
- Open with the sharpest line (the hook) — no warm-up, no "as you know."
- Show, then tell — a real transcript / a real bug / a real number beats any claim.
- One idea per post. One audience per post.
- End with a clear, low-friction CTA (and a `?ref=` link).
- Use the credibility doctrine: *verify free, fail privately, share only what you're proud of.*

## Don't
- Don't say "deleted Horizon." We **lead with VELO; Horizon rolls in.** (Narrative must match the code.)
- Don't over-claim ("the truth machine"). Say "evidence you can re-judge."
- Don't mock anyone. Don't use 5 hashtags. Don't write LinkedIn-broetry ("Agree? 👇").
- Don't bury the lede or pad. If a sentence isn't earning its place, cut it.

## Vocabulary
- **Use:** proof of work · defend your work · reads your actual code · the architect vs. the prompter ·
  reality check · where you actually stand · output is free · a credential you're proud to share.
- **Avoid:** revolutionary · game-changing · seamless · unlock · synergy · "excited to share."

## The one-liner positioning
> *VELO is how good developers prove they're the real thing in a world of AI fakes — a credential you're
> proud to share, not a test you're afraid to take. It's the critical core of Horizon; the learning
> layer rolls out as you ask "how do I close the gap?"*

## Your script, two ways

### A) Long founder / explainer version (LinkedIn text or 2–3 min video) — keeps your substance, fixed frame
> I spent a year building Horizon, and I noticed something late: most of it is a *nice-to-have.* A better
> way to learn. Genuinely useful — but people can live without it.
>
> The part that isn't optional sits inside it: **VELO** — a verification system that checks whether
> someone can actually back up their resume. Not "did they list a project," but "can they defend it" —
> across their projects, experience and certifications.
>
> Why it matters, for everyone in the loop:
> • **HR** finally knows if a candidate can really pull off what the job needs — before wasting an interview.
> • **Placement cells** see how many students are actually career-ready, and exactly what training they need.
> • **Institutions** can shape syllabus and training around real gaps, not guesses.
> • **Students** get an honest reality check: where they actually stand, which skills they really have,
>   whether it matches the job they want — and what to do next to get there.
>
> That last part is the whole point. VELO tells you the truth; then it points you at the next step.
>
> So I'm leading with VELO. The rest of Horizon rolls in as you ask "okay — how do I close the gap?"
> Building it in public. Get verified free — you only share it if you're proud of it.

### B) Punchy Reel version (~30s, hook-first, show-don't-tell)
- **[0–3s]:** "Your resume says you built it. Can you actually defend it?"
- **[3–10s] screen-rec:** VELO reading the repo → a question citing a real file appears.
- **[10–22s]:** a hard adaptive follow-up after a vague answer. Caption: "It read your code. It adapts. You can't fake this."
- **[22–28s]:** verdict seal animates to a score. Caption: "A credential you're proud to share."
- **[28–30s] CTA:** "Verify free — link in bio." (`?ref=reel_pitch`)

**Why B beats your original for social:** hook in 3s, shows the magic, one idea, one CTA. Save the
4-audience depth (A) for LinkedIn/long video where it lands.
