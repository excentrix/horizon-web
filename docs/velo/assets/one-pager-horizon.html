Horizon is an adaptive AI mentorship platform that builds a living model of each learner — goals, gaps, schedule, pace and learning style — and turns it into a daily plan. It combines an AI mentor you can talk to, diagnostic skips, spaced repetition, and a verified skill portfolio in one product.

1

One system instead of four. Horizon replaces the usual patchwork of random courses, generic chatbots, disconnected notes, and portfolio guesswork with a single loop: understand the learner, decide what comes next, help them do the work, and publish proof of skill they can actually show.

How Horizon works

  1. 01

    Model the learner

    A short onboarding conversation and diagnostic build a persistent model of goals, skills, constraints, and pace.

  2. 02

    Generate today’s plan

    Horizon turns that model into a daily path — what to study, what to skip, what to review, and what to build next.

  3. 03

    Turn work into proof

    Every task ties to artifacts, projects, analyses, and verified portfolio evidence that can be shown to employers or institutions.
