Incubation and campus collaboration proposal

Request for incubation status, office space, and a student talent pipeline at GAT.

Excentrix is a Bangalore-based technology venture developing AI-enabled proof-of-work and skill-verification systems for education and industry. Founded by alumni of Global Academy of Technology, we seek formal incubation, dedicated workspace, and a structured internship partnership with GAT to establish a durable industry-academia collaboration on campus.

01

Founder statement and institutional intent. As an alumnus of Global Academy of Technology, returning to GAT to incubate Excentrix represents an opportunity to convert alumni experience into institutional value. The aim is to bring live product development, applied AI engineering, and sustained industry engagement back to campus in a form that is visible, practical, and beneficial to students.

Why this matters now
Friction 01

Technical learning still lacks credible evidence

Students complete substantial work, yet institutions often lack a dependable method to convert that work into portable and verifiable proof of capability.

Institutional gap: work exists, but evidence remains fragmented.
Friction 02

AI has weakened traditional hiring signals

Polished portfolios, resumes, and repositories are now easier to generate at scale. Establishing genuine ownership and applied depth has become materially harder.

Institutional gap: presentation quality no longer confirms capability.
Friction 03

Campus-industry integration remains limited

Students benefit most from exposure to real product environments, while institutions increasingly need measurable, industry-linked outcomes.

Institutional gap: stronger continuity is needed between campus and practice.
Founder and alumni context
Siddharth Karthikeyan, Founder & CEO of Excentrix, is a B.E. Artificial Intelligence & Data Science alumnus of Global Academy of Technology. The objective is not merely to operate from campus, but to establish a functional bridge between academic rigor and real-world technology deployment through live products, active engineering work, structured internships, workshops, and an alumni-led innovation presence within GAT.
